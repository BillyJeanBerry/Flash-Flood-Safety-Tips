To prepare for a flash flood, you should check your area if it has any floods. Then you should create a emergency kit, consisting of water, with a gallon of water per day per person. Have enough non perishable food for several days. A flashlight and a radio. You should also create first aid kit, along with adding any medications you or your family/friends might need. To make sure your important documents don't get ruined during the flood, you should put them in a safe space, and even create copies just in case. Now that you prepared yourself, you should prepare your property. Make sure your gutters and storm drains have no debris in them, if you also have any important objects that are om the ground or low level area, you should move them to a higher place. If you have them, use plastic tarps and sand bags to keep out the water. If you ever needed to evacaute, make a go bag that has important docuemnts, medications, food, water, and flashlights. To plan for a flash flood, you should always know the best escape routes. If you have friends or family, you should always plan with them so you can meet up with the safetly. You should also plan what to do if you are ever on the road while there is a flash fload. When you are in your car, look for high ground immediately, do not go through any flood water, as it can be deeper than it looks. If you are trapped in your car, you should go on top of the roof of it. Make sure to listen the radio or any trusted news source and never go close to the flood or streets. If you are trapped in a building, move to the highest level, however make sure you can't be trapped by water if it rises.
