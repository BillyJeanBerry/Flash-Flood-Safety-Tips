To plan for a flash flood, you should always know the best escape routes. If you have friends or family, you should always plan with them so you can meet up with the safety. You should also plan what to do if you are ever on the road while there is a flash flood. When you are in your car, look for high ground immediately, do not go through any flood water, as it can be deeper than it looks. If you are trapped in your car, you should go on top of the roof of it. Make sure to listen the radio or any trusted news source and never go close to the flood or streets. If you are trapped in a building, move to the highest level, however make sure you can't be trapped by water if it rises.
