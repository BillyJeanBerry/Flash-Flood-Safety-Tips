To prepare for a flash flood, you should check your area if it has any floods. Then you should create a emergency kit, consisting of water, with a gallon of water per day per person. Have enough non perishable food for several days. A flashlight and a radio. You should also create first aid kit, along with adding any medications you or your family/friends might need. To make sure your important documents don't get ruined during the flood, you should put them in a safe space, and even create copies just in case. Now that you prepared yourself, you should prepare your property. Make sure your gutters and storm drains have no debris in them, if you also have any important objects that are om the ground or low level area, you should move them to a higher place. If you have them, use plastic tarps and sand bags to keep out the water. If you ever needed to evacuate, make a go bag that has important documents, medications, food, water, and flashlights.
